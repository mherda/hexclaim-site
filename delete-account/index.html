Delete your Hexclaim account

Hexclaim, by Marcin Herda

  • In the app, Delete account deletes your account and everything we hold about you straight away.
  • Without the app, email us from your Google account's address and we'll delete it within 30 days.

In the app

  • Android: Journal › Settings › Account › Delete account.
  • iPhone: Settings › Account › Delete account.

Either deletes everything straight away.

Without the app

Email privacy@hexclaim.space from the address of the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Hexclaim account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

iPhone accounts hold no email address, so on iPhone delete your account in the app. If you can't, email us and we'll help.

What is deleted

  • Your account, with the Google email address, name and picture link from Google sign-in.
  • Your display names.
  • The groups you own, with everything in them.
  • Your memberships in other groups and your weekly hexes.
  • Push tokens and invites.

Nothing is kept afterwards, except that our database provider's backups may hold a copy for a short time until they expire.

What isn't on our server

Your hexes, walks and badges stay on your phone and in your own Google backup or iCloud, which we can't read. Uninstall the app to remove them from your phone, and manage the backup in your phone's backup or iCloud settings.

Crash reports are not linked to you, and Sentry deletes them within 90 days.
